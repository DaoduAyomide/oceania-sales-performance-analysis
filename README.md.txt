# Oceania Cargo Chain Tracker

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the sales and profitability performance of Oceania Cargo Chain.

The dashboard was developed to provide an overview of sales, profit, customers, orders, profit margin, shipping modes, regions, product categories, and order priorities.

The objective was to transform business data into an interactive dashboard that can help management understand sales performance and identify areas that require attention.

## Business Questions

The analysis was designed to answer the following questions:

- What are the total sales and total profit?
- What is the overall profit margin?
- How many customers and orders were recorded?
- Which shipping modes contribute most to sales?
- Which regions generate the highest sales?
- How does performance vary across product categories?
- How does order priority relate to discounts?
- Which product sub-categories contribute most to sales and profit margin?

## Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard provides visual analysis of:

- Sales by shipping mode
- Sales and profit performance
- Discounts by order priority
- Sales and profit margin by product sub-category
- Regional performance
- Customer performance
- Product category performance

Interactive filters allow the data to be explored by:
- Ship Mode
- Region
- Customer Name
- Product Category
- Order Priority

## Key Insights

The dashboard provides a high-level view of Oceania Cargo Chain's sales and profitability performance.

It allows users to identify differences in performance across shipping modes, regions, product categories, customers, and order priorities.

The analysis also highlights the relationship between discounts and order priority, providing an additional perspective for evaluating pricing and sales strategies. The following KPI's were visualized which are the Total Sales  ($14,915,600.82), Total Profit ($1,521,767.98), Total Customers (8,699), Total Orders 214,777.


## Recommendations

Based on the analysis, management should:

1. Focus on high-performing shipping modes and regions.
2. Identify product categories and sub-categories contributing strongly to sales and profitability.
3. Review discount strategies across different order priorities.
4. Monitor customer performance to identify high-value customers.
5. Use regional and product-level informations to improve sales planning.
